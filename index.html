Production Standards

The All Into Art Pipeline

A disciplined, milestone-driven workflow ensuring visual excellence and engine compatibility at every stage.

01

Concept & Blockout

Silhouette definition, proportion blockouts, reference boards, and grid alignment testing to establish accurate scale before detailed modeling.

02

High-Poly & Retopo

High-frequency micro-sculpting in ZBrush followed by manual retopology in Maya, ensuring optimal edge flow across joints and facial regions.

03

PBR Texturing

Normal and curvature baking in Marmoset Toolbag, followed by multi-layered PBR texture painting in Substance 3D Painter with realistic wear.

04

Engine Integration

Asset import into Unreal Engine 5, configuring master material instances, physics assets, collision boundaries, and real-time lighting passes.

About The Studio

Where Craft Meets Game Reality

All Into Art is a game development and 3D digital art company founded by Haider Shaikh (Karachi, Pakistan / Global Remote).

Specializing in 3D generalist art, character modeling, environment creation, and Unreal Engine 5 real-time production, Haider works alongside indie game creators and commercial studios worldwide to bring complex game worlds and expressive characters to life.

Every asset delivered by All Into Art is built from the ground up to satisfy both aesthetic ambition and rigorous engine performance standards.
