Core Capabilities

Full-Cycle Game Development Services

From initial concept sculpts to shipping-grade Unreal Engine 5 levels, All Into Art provides end-to-end 3D art and engineering solutions.

Game Development & Prototyping

Rapid gameplay prototyping in Unreal Engine 5 utilizing Blueprints, C++, and custom component architecture for responsive third-person, action, and RPG titles.

  • Core Player Controller & Movement Mechanics
  • Camera Shake, Combat Logic & Hit Reactions
  • Inventory, Stats & Game State Architecture

Unreal Engine 5 Environments

Breath-taking world building powered by Nanite, Lumen GI, and World Partition. Modular architecture kits, terrain sculpting, and cinematic lighting setups.

  • Lumen Dynamic Lighting & Reflection Tweaking
  • Atmospheric Volumetric Clouds & Niagara Fog
  • Modular Sci-Fi, Gothic & Realistic Kits

3D Character & Creature Art

Organic and hard-surface characters from high-poly ZBrush sculpts to game-ready low-poly models with clean edge flow for seamless deformation.

  • High-Poly ZBrush Sculpting & Anatomy
  • Game-Ready Retopology & UV Packing
  • Marvelous Designer Realistic Cloth Simulation

Rigging & Combat Animation

Custom bipedal and quadruped skeletal rigs with IK/FK switching, blendspaces, and visceral combat animation loops that feel punchy in-engine.

  • UE5 Control Rig & Full-Body IK Setup
  • Keyframe Combat Animations & Transitions
  • Motion Warping & Sequencer Cutscene Directing

Hard-Surface Props & Vehicles

Precision hard-surface modeling for weapons, mechs, military vehicles, and intricate sci-fi props, textured in Substance 3D Painter with PBR fidelity.

  • Sub-D & Boolean Hard-Surface Modeling
  • Weathering, Scratches & Material Layering
  • Rigged Weapon Skeletal Meshes with Socket Points

Technical Art & Optimization

Keeping your game running at a rock-solid 60 FPS. Draw call reduction, texture atlas packing, channel packing (ORM maps), and custom HLSL shader optimization.

  • VRAM Footprint & Draw Call Profiling
  • Collision Hulls, Nanite Fallbacks & LOD Chains
  • Master Material Architecture & Dynamic Instances
Production Standards

The All Into Art Pipeline

A battle-tested production sequence refined across dozens of indie and commercial game titles.

01

Concept & Pre-Viz

Defining art direction, visual benchmarks, grid scale metrics, silhouette blockouts, and reference moodboards before polygon work begins.

02

High-Poly & Topology

Sculpting intricate micro-details in ZBrush, followed by clean game-ready retopology in Maya with deliberate edge loops around deformation points.

03

PBR Material Mastery

Baking high-to-low normal and curvature maps in Marmoset Toolbag, followed by multi-layered PBR texture painting in Substance 3D Painter.

04

UE5 Engine Integration

Importing assets into Unreal Engine 5, configuring Nanite, master material instances, physics assets, collision meshes, and real-time lighting.

About The Studio

Where Vision Meets Real-Time Innovation

All Into Art is a passionate indie game development studio and 3D digital art venture helmed by Haider Shaikh. With an extensive background in 3D generalist art, real-time Unreal Engine 5 production, character modeling, and environment design, our studio empowers game developers and creative directors to translate ambitious concepts into breathtaking, playable realities.

We bridge the gap between high-concept artistic imagination and disciplined game engineering. Every polygon, light bounce, and texture atlas we construct is precision-tailored to run smoothly at 60 FPS while commanding the visual authority of a AAA title.

Unreal Engine 5 Specialist

Mastery of Lumen, Nanite, World Partition, Niagara VFX, and Sequencer.

End-to-End Asset Delivery

High-poly sculpts down to rigged, weighted, and LOD-optimized engine assets.

Global Remote Collaboration

Reliable communication, rapid iteration cycles, and milestone-driven delivery.

Indie & Commercial Experience

Proudly contributing to game dev communities and studio productions worldwide.
